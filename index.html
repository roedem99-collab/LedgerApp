How to Export from USAA
  1. Log in to usaa.com
  2. Go to My Accounts → Checking / Savings
  3. Click Download Activity (or "Export")
  4. Choose CSV format and your date range
  5. Save the file, then drag it into the Import CSV button above
🔒 Your data never leaves this file. Everything is stored locally in your browser on this computer only.
